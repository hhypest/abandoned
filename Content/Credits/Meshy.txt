Included in the game:
DEV-01A, DEV-01B, DEV-02, DEV-03, DEV-04, DEV-05, DEV-06, DEV-07, DEV-12, DEV-13, EQP-00, EQP-01, EQP-05.
The assets were imported into Unreal Engine and placed at gameplay scale.
DEV-02 was split into a housing and moving plate; the cut edges were capped.
The EQP-01 strap was removed and the cut openings were capped.
The earlier first-person glove setup mirrored EQP-05 for the right hand.

The game copy of EQP-00 adds a skeleton and skinning for the body, hands and legs.
The sculpted terminal was removed. A mirrored section of the original right sleeve was stitched to the left wrist and elbow collars. EQP-01 attaches to the left forearm and carries the game display.
The EQP-00 and EQP-01 game materials combine the original PBR textures with a project-authored fine surface finish. The display and its typography are rendered in-game.
The 13 static meshes include Unreal Nanite geometry with explicit tangents. Their original textures and gameplay collision are preserved.
